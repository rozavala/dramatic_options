## The gap — why the agency feed will not supply this channel

The dated events that would move the universe's theses are mostly not in the Federal Register, or not
scheduled there:

- **MRK / BNTX-class oncology**: FDA decision dates (PDUFA goals, advisory committees). There is no FDA channel.
- **Defense primes, KTOS**: DoD contract announcements. There is no DoD or SAM.gov channel.
- **CC (refrigerant transition)**: the EPA AIM Act technology-transition compliance dates. **Nothing** on
  them appeared in W35–W38.
- **Nuclear names**: NRC licensing decisions are usually unscheduled in the Federal Register; the NRC's own
  review schedules are published on nrc.gov, not in the Register.
- **VIAV / BEAD**: construction starts appear only in newsletters and trade press, and never with an ISO date
  (the standing class-(c) blocker).
